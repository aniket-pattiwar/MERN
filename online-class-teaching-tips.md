# Tips and Tricks for Teaching During Online Class
## Audience: College Students | Format: Short Course

1. Set clear outcomes for each session.
- Start every class with 2-3 learning goals.
- End with a quick recap tied to those goals.

2. Keep lessons short and focused.
- Use 10-15 minute teaching blocks.
- Break long topics into mini-modules.

3. Use active learning every class.
- Add polls, quick quizzes, or short discussions every 10 minutes.
- Ask students to explain ideas in their own words.

4. Build a predictable class routine.
- Use a fixed structure: warm-up, concept, activity, reflection.
- Students engage more when they know what comes next.

5. Increase participation with low-pressure prompts.
- Use chat responses, reaction emojis, or one-line answers.
- Call on students gently and rotate fairly.

6. Design practical, real-world tasks.
- Give small case studies and role-based problems.
- Connect assignments to careers and internships.

7. Make instructions and deadlines simple.
- Share one-page weekly plans.
- Use clear rubrics and examples for assignments.

8. Support attention and energy.
- Include short breaks in longer sessions.
- Use visual variety: slides, whiteboard, demos.

9. Give fast and useful feedback.
- Return short feedback quickly on key mistakes.
- Use audio/video comments for clarity when needed.

10. Keep communication open.
- Hold brief office hours.
- Use one main channel for announcements and questions.

11. Promote teamwork.
- Use breakout rooms with clear roles and time limits.
- Ask groups to submit one shared output.

12. Track progress and adjust.
- Check weekly attendance, quiz scores, and participation.
- Re-teach difficult points before moving on.

## Quick Weekly Template (Short Course)
- Monday: Introduce concept + mini activity
- Wednesday: Practice + peer discussion
- Friday: Quiz + feedback + next-week preview
